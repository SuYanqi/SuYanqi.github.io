About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Software Engineering & AI at Technische Universität München (TUM), working with Prof. Chunyang Chen. My research interests lie at the intersection of Software Engineering, Knowledge Graph, and Large Language Models. I received my Doctor degree in Computer Science from the Australian National University in Australia, advised by Prof. Zhenchang Xing. My current research focus is software testing including the following four directions.

Research Interest

  • Exploratory Software Testing
  • Change-Aware Testing
  • Software Repository Mining
  • Multimodal Large Language Models
